## Delivery & Shipping Policy

This page explains how your order gets from our store to you.

### Where we deliver

- **Dhaka.** Home or office delivery within Dhaka city.
- **Outside Dhaka.** Delivery through courier services to districts across Bangladesh.
- **Showroom pickup.** You can collect your order from our showroom at 85, Laboratory Road, New Elephant Road. Ask for pickup when we call to confirm, and bring your order number and a photo ID.

### How long it takes

| Area | Typical delivery time |
|---|---|
| Inside Dhaka | 1–3 working days |
| Outside Dhaka (courier) | 2–7 working days |

The clock starts when we confirm your order, not when you place it. After confirmation we usually dispatch within 1–2 working days. Pre-order, upcoming and made-to-order items follow the timeframe shown on the product page. Working days exclude public holidays.

### Delivery charges

Your delivery charge is calculated at checkout and shown before you pay. It depends on the delivery area and the size and weight of the parcel. Where we run a free-delivery offer, it is stated on the product page or in the campaign banner. The charge you see at checkout is the charge you pay.

### Order confirmation

After you place an order, we send an SMS with your order number and call the phone number you gave us to confirm the details. If we cannot reach you, we may hold the order until we do. For high-value orders sent by courier outside Dhaka, we may ask for an advance payment; we will tell you the amount when we call.

### Paying for your order

Available payment options appear at checkout and may include cash on delivery, mobile payment, cards and other online payment. Some options may not be available for every product or area. See the [FAQ](/faq) for more on payment.

### Tracking your order

Use our [order tracking page](/track) with your order number, or call us. You can follow your order on the tracking page at any time.

### When your parcel arrives

Please check the parcel before you accept it:

1. Look at the outer packaging. If the box is torn, wet or visibly damaged, do not accept the parcel and tell us straight away.
2. Check that the product and the model match your order.
3. Record a short video as you open the box. It is the best evidence if something is wrong and speeds up any claim.

Report a wrong or visibly damaged item within **24 hours** of delivery. Full details are in our [Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy](/returns).

### Delays

Weather, public holidays, courier problems or stock arriving late can delay delivery. If your order is running late we will let you know and update you on the new date.

We want you to be happy with what you buy. This policy covers cancelling an order, reporting a problem on delivery and getting a replacement or refund. For faults that appear after the first few days, see our [Warranty Policy](/warranty).

### Reporting a problem on delivery

| Problem | Tell us within |
|---|---|
| Wrong item, missing item or visible damage from transit | 24 hours of delivery |
| Dead on arrival (the product does not work out of the box) | 3 days of delivery |

1. Contact us on 01611-305338 or support@technohouse.com.bd with your order number and a description of the problem. Attach your unboxing video or photos if you have them.

We inspect the returned product. If it meets the conditions:

- Products damaged after delivery, including physical, liquid, fire or electrical damage

- Software, licences and digital codes
- Reports made after the periods above (these may still be covered by warranty)

## Warranty Policy

Most products we sell come with a warranty from the brand or its importer. This page explains how warranty works when you buy from Techno House and how to make a claim.

### How long is my warranty?

- The warranty period is stated on your **invoice** and on the product page. The invoice is what counts.
- Only products with a warranty period written on the invoice are covered.
- A replacement unit keeps the original purchase date; it does not start a new warranty period.
- For "lifetime" warranty offers, the brand's own terms apply. If a model is discontinued, coverage may end as the brand specifies.

### What is covered

Manufacturing defects that appear under normal use during the warranty period. For a covered fault, we repair the product, or arrange repair through the brand's service centre.

- **Repair first.** Repair is the standard remedy.
- **Replacement.** If the product cannot be repaired, we replace it with the same model.
- **Alternative or refund.** If the same model is not available, we offer an equal-value alternative, or a refund if no alternative suits you.

### How to make a claim

1. Collect your invoice, the product and all its accessories. Keep the original box if you still have it.
2. Back up your data and sign out of personal accounts (email, banking, social media, device locks such as Apple ID or Google account). We cannot be responsible for data on a device.
3. Visit our RMA office: 85/1, 2nd Floor, Laboratory Road (Coffee House Goli), New Elephant Road, Dhaka-1205. Call 01311-211433 first if you want to check that someone is available.
4. We examine the product, record the problem and give you a claim receipt.
5. We tell you the outcome: repair, replacement or service-centre referral.

Outside Dhaka, contact us before sending anything. We will advise you on how to ship the product safely. Courier cost to us is normally paid by the customer.

### How long does it take?

It depends on the fault, the brand and whether parts are in stock. Some repairs take a few days. Others take several weeks if parts must be ordered from abroad. We will keep you updated and give an estimate when we receive the product.

### What is not covered

- Physical damage, drops, cracks and burns
- Liquid damage
- Damage from power surges, wrong voltage or non-original adapters
- Products that have been opened, repaired or modified by anyone other than an authorised service centre
- A missing, damaged or altered serial number or warranty sticker
- Normal wear, such as battery ageing, scratches and worn keys
- Consumables and free accessories unless the invoice says otherwise, for example cables, adapters, mice, headphones, ink, toner and cartridges
- Software problems, passwords, locked accounts and data loss
- Use outside the manufacturer's specifications
- Incomplete products
- Brand-specific exclusions, such as a display's dead-pixel limits, which follow the brand's own policy

### Brand service centres

For some brands the claim is handled directly at the brand's own service centre. When that applies, we will tell you where to go and help you with the paperwork.
