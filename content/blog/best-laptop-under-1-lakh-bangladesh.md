## Things people forget to check

- **Upgrade options.** Is there a spare M.2 slot? Can the RAM be replaced?
- **Screen quality.** Brightness and colour accuracy matter if you work outside or edit photos.
- **Charging.** USB-C charging is convenient, but not every laptop has it.
