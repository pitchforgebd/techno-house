# Best Laptop for Students in Bangladesh

The best student laptop is the one that matches your subject and survives being carried around every day. A BBA student and a CSE or architecture student do not need the same machine, so start with your course.

## Match it to your subject

**General studies, business and humanities.** Word processing, slides, research and online classes need little power. Choose reliability, battery life and a good keyboard.

**Computer science and engineering.** Programming tools and virtual machines like plenty of memory. Check your department's software requirements before you buy [VERIFY: suggest a RAM target from current stock].

**Architecture, design and media.** Software like AutoCAD, Revit or Premiere often benefits from a dedicated graphics card [VERIFY: check current system requirements for each named program]. Ask your seniors which programs they actually use.

## What matters for every student

- **An SSD.** It makes the laptop feel quick for years. Please do not buy a hard disk model.
- **Battery life.** Campus classrooms do not always have free sockets, and load shedding happens [VERIFY: replace with real battery figures if naming models].
- **Weight and build.** It will spend a lot of time in a backpack on a crowded bus.
- **A webcam and microphone** that work properly for online classes.

## Save money sensibly

You usually do not need the newest model. A slightly older laptop with a good specification can be better value than a new one with a weak specification. Also check whether a student version of the software you need is available from your university, as that can save you money.
