## Decide: graphics card now, or later?

You have two sensible routes.

**Route 1: buy a graphics card now.** Choose a modest processor and put more of the budget towards the graphics card. You get better gaming performance from day one.

**Route 2: start with a processor that has built-in graphics.** Play lighter games now and add a graphics card when you have saved up [VERIFY: name processors in stock with usable integrated graphics]. Choose a power supply and casing that will still suit that future card.

## Parts that protect the rest

Spend carefully on the power supply, because this is the worst place to save money. A reliable unit protects every other part in the system [VERIFY: suggest a wattage for the final example build].

Choose memory that matches the motherboard. Two matched sticks generally perform better than one on platforms with dual channel [VERIFY: suggest a capacity for this budget]. An NVMe SSD is worth it even at this budget, and you can always add more storage later.

## Where you can save

- A plain casing with reasonable airflow instead of a flashy one
- The stock cooler, if the processor includes one and you are not overclocking
- A basic motherboard, as long as it has the socket and slots you need
