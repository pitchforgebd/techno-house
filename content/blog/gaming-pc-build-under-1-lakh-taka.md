## Spend on the graphics card first

The graphics card has the biggest effect on games. A common starting point is to give it the largest share of the budget, around a third or a little more [VERIFY: check this share against current part prices].

## Match the processor, do not overbuy

A mid-range processor is usually plenty for gaming at this level. Pairing a very expensive processor with a modest graphics card leaves frame rates limited by the graphics card anyway [VERIFY: name a sensible processor tier from current stock].

## Choose a platform you can upgrade

Check the motherboard socket, because it decides which processors you can move to later. Also confirm whether the board uses DDR4 or DDR5 memory, since they are not interchangeable.
