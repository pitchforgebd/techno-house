# Best Laptop Under 50,000 Taka in Bangladesh

Fifty thousand taka [VERIFY: confirm this is still a realistic laptop budget in current stock] is the budget most students and first-time buyers ask us about. You will not get everything at this price, so the trick is knowing what to protect and what you can give up.

## What to protect

**An SSD, always.** A laptop with an SSD feels quicker every single day than one with a hard disk, even if the processor is the same. If you find a model with a hard disk at this budget, keep looking.

**Enough memory.** Aim for at least 8GB of RAM [VERIFY: confirm 8GB is the common baseline in this price range]. With less than that, a browser with a few tabs and Microsoft Word will start to feel heavy. Check whether the RAM can be upgraded later, because some thin models have it soldered in.

**A recent processor.** Do not judge by the "Core i5" or "Ryzen 5" name alone. An older i5 can be slower than a newer i3, so check the generation in the model number [VERIFY: give one current example from stock].

## What you can give up

- A dedicated graphics card. At this budget it usually means cutting back elsewhere, and integrated graphics are fine for study and office work.
- A very slim body. A slightly thicker laptop often costs less for the same performance.
- A high refresh rate screen, unless you play fast games.

## Check these before paying

Look at the screen resolution, since a low-resolution panel makes text look rough: Full HD is worth having if you can get it [VERIFY: confirm Full HD availability at this budget]. Then look at the battery rating and the weight if you will carry it to campus every day. Bangladesh-e load shedding er somoy battery ta kaje lage, so do not ignore this.

Finally, check the warranty on the product page and keep your invoice.
