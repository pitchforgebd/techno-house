Choosing a laptop gets easier once you decide what you will really do on it. A student writing assignments and joining online classes needs something very different from a freelancer editing video late at night, or someone who wants to play games after work. So before comparing models, ask yourself one honest question: **ei laptop ta diye ami asole ki korbo?**

For everyday study and office work, look for a recent processor, enough memory to keep a browser and a few apps open, and an SSD rather than a hard disk. Battery life and weight matter more than people expect if you carry it to campus or on a bus every day. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which is better for a student, an SSD or a hard disk?
An SSD. It makes starting the laptop and opening apps noticeably faster, and it copes better with being carried around every day.

### Is a gaming laptop a good choice for office work?
It works, but it is usually heavier and has shorter battery life than a regular laptop. If you do not need the graphics power, a lighter model will be easier to live with.
