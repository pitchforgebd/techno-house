A desktop still makes a lot of sense when the computer stays in one place. You generally get more performance for the money than from a laptop, it is easier to repair, and you can replace one part at a time instead of the whole machine. For a shop counter, an office desk or a family computer at home, that long life is hard to beat.

The right type depends on how you work. A [brand PC](/category/brand-pc) comes as one tested system with the manufacturer's support, which many offices prefer. An [all-in-one PC](/category/all-in-one-pc) keeps the desk tidy because the computer sits inside the screen. A [mini PC](/category/portable-mini-pc) suits small spaces and simple tasks. If you need graphics power for games, go straight to our [gaming PCs](/category/gaming-pc), and if you like Apple's software, see the [Mac mini](/category/apple-mac-mini) range.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a desktop better than a laptop for office work?
If the computer stays on one desk, usually yes: it is easier to upgrade and repair. If people need to carry it to meetings or home, a laptop is the practical choice.

### What is the difference between a brand PC and a custom PC?
A brand PC is built and supported as one product by its manufacturer. A custom PC is assembled from parts you choose, which gives you more control over each component.

### Do I need a UPS with a desktop?
A desktop has no battery, so it shuts off the moment the power goes. A UPS gives you time to save your work and shut down properly.
