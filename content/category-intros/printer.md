The purchase price of a printer is only part of what it costs you. Ink or toner goes on costing money for as long as you own it, so start by estimating how much you really print, and whether it is mostly black text or colour pages.

Ink tank printers suit homes, students and small shops that print regularly, because you refill bottles rather than replacing cartridges. Laser printers are a good match for offices that produce a lot of black and white documents, since toner does not dry out between jobs and pages come out quickly. If you print rarely, remember that ink can dry out in the nozzles when a machine sits unused for weeks. ## Frequently asked questions

### Should I buy an ink tank or a laser printer?
Ink tank printers suit regular colour printing, while laser printers suit high volumes of black and white documents.

### Will ink dry out if I do not print often?
It can. Printing a page now and then helps keep an inkjet's nozzles clear. A laser printer is a safer choice if it will sit unused for long periods.

### Can I print from my phone?
Many WiFi models support it. Check the connectivity listed on the product page of the model you are considering.
