The processor sets the pace for the whole computer, but the most expensive one is not automatically the right one. Everyday office work and browsing do not need many cores. Video editing, 3D work and software development benefit from more of them. Most games care about strong performance on a smaller number of cores, along with a graphics card that matches.

Here at Techno House you will mainly be choosing between AMD Ryzen and Intel Core processors [VERIFY: current processor brands in stock]. Rather than picking a side, compare the actual models available within your budget and read what they are good at.

The most important check is compatibility, and it is where most first-time builders go wrong. A processor only works with a motherboard that has the matching socket and a supporting chipset. Also check which type of memory the platform uses, because DDR4 and DDR5 are not interchangeable. ## Frequently asked questions

### Will this processor fit my motherboard?
Only if the socket matches and the chipset supports it. Check the socket shown on both product pages, or use the PC Builder.

### Does every processor include a cooler?
No. Check the product page, and buy a suitable CPU cooler separately if one is not included.

### Do I need a separate graphics card?
That depends on the processor. Some have built-in graphics that are fine for office work. Others have none, so a graphics card is required just to get a picture on the screen.
